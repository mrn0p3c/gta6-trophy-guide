    Roadmap to the Platinum

    Six stages, built from the official trophy descriptions: clear the story first, watch the five missables while the campaign is still running, then sweep activities, collectibles and the money counters before the 100% audit. Estimated shape of the run: one story playthrough, no difficulty trophy, no online trophies on the official list.

    Missable trophies — read before you play

    Five trophies are flagged missable, each flagged from its official description: a one-time location visit, a set-piece exit, a partnership moment, a counter built on choices, and one that needs a failed first attempt. Everything else on the list can be cleaned up after the credits. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and none of the five can hurt you.
